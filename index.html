Conception sonore complète pour l'image : effets, ambiances, textures. Chaque élément est créé sur mesure pour révéler l'intention narrative.

Assemblage, synchronisation et traitement de la bande son. Chaque élément est à sa place, pour une lisibilité claire et un rythme maîtrisé.

Captation terrain et bruitage sur mesure. Des textures organiques uniques, créées pour le projet, jamais issues de banques standards.

Création d'identités sonores : logo audio, jingle, habillage. Une signature cohérente, pensée pour marquer durablement l'écoute.

Glossary

Time Locked
The edit is finalized before sound work begins. Picture length and cut points are fixed, no changes allowed during production to avoid costly re-syncing.
Sound Editing
The process of assembling and synchronizing all audio elements to picture, dialogue, ambiences, effects, frame by frame.
SFX
Sound Effects. Designed or recorded sounds that bring life to on-screen events: impacts, atmospheres, UI sounds, transitions.
Foley
Recreated everyday sounds performed and recorded in sync with the image, footsteps, cloth, props. Named after pioneer Jack Foley.
Voice Processing
Cleaning, equalizing and placing recorded dialogue into the sonic environment of the project. Includes noise removal and perspective matching.
Mastering
Final stage of audio processing that optimizes the mix for a specific distribution format, loudness, dynamic range, and frequency balance.
Stems
Separate audio exports grouped by category: dialogue stem, music stem, effects stem. Allows precise control during broadcast or dubbing.
Format Specs
Technical requirements per distribution channel. Web (−14 LUFS stereo), Broadcast (−23 LUFS, 5.1), Cinema (−20 LUFS, Dolby Atmos) each have distinct loudness and codec standards.
Preview
An intermediate export sent to the client or director during production. Allows validation of creative choices before the final mix, avoids costly last-minute changes.
Linear
Working method where audio is edited and synchronized directly against the locked picture, from start to finish, in real time with the image.
